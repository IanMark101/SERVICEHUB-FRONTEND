# Activity information architecture direction

## Direction contract

THESIS: Activity is a responsibility-first overview, not a grid of equally prominent booking cards. A compact single-column feed leads with Your Turn, then Work Underway, Waiting, contextual Under Review, and History. Selecting an item opens one focused workroom.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve ServiceHub's existing shell, typography, light/dark themes, Seeker orange and Provider green. Use restrained neutrals and thin grouping lines, with color reserved for current decisions and real status.

STORY: Within seconds, either participant can tell what is happening, whether they must act, and what happens next. Payment, queue, and booking states remain separately named. Completed and canceled bookings recede into compact history.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Beneath the familiar workspace header, show search, sort, and status filter, followed immediately by Your Turn entries when present. Rows contain a title, participant, plain-language state, next step, and a clear Open control. The workroom leads with Now, Your Action, and Next; facts and a secondary journey follow.

FORM: Approved Concept A overview combined with Concept B focused detail. The signature interaction is an accessible row-to-workroom transition that preserves the overview's filter, search, sort, and scroll context. No backend lifecycle or business behavior changes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
